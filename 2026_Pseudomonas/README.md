# 2026 Pseudmonas Project

The study of Xue et al., 2026 explores alternative biocontrol strategies against multidrug‑resistant *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*, a major spoilage organism in aquatic food products. I chose this topic for my analysis because it illustrates the ecological adaptability and resilience of *Pseudomonas*, making it a relevant model for understanding resistance, virulence, and biocontrol potential. It also connects well to genomic analysis by showing how bacterial traits influence treatment outcomes.

All the data are from this article: Siming Xue, Kairui Zhao, Rui Dong, Haichen Li, Ruizi Wang, Wei Cheng, Yuqi Fan, Qin Zhang, Ziyi Xue, Yujin Wu, Jun Zhang, Yanmei Sun, Shiwei Wang, Synergistic control of Pseudomonas aeruginosa by LAB and phage for sustainable aquatic preservation, Food Bioscience, Volume 77, 2026, 108408, ISSN 2212-4292, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fbio.2026.108408.

# Sequence data are available at NCBI (NCBI BioGroup:PRJNA877469).
