# 2026 Aeromonas Project

The study by Lee et al. 2023 investigates *Aeromonas* species using whole‑genome sequencing to clarify their taxonomy, virulence potential, and antimicrobial resistance profiles. The authors show that *Aeromonas* genomes display high genetic diversity, frequent horizontal gene transfer, and clinically relevant resistance determinants. Their work highlights how genome‑based classification improves the identification of pathogenic strains and supports more accurate epidemiological tracking. I chose to analyze *Aeromonas* genomes because their genetic variability and environmental adaptability make them ideal for studying evolutionary processes and resistance mechanisms. 


All the data are from this article: Lee H-J, Storesund JE, Lunestad B-T, Hoel S, Lerfall J and Jakobsen AN (2023) Whole genome sequence analysis of *Aeromonas* spp. isolated from ready-to-eat seafood: antimicrobial resistance and virulence factors. Front. Microbiol. 14:1175304. doi: 10.3389/fmicb.2023.1175304
